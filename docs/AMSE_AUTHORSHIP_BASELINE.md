## Statements of record

1. **The poster roster above is the intended author team for the
   eventual AMSE publication.**
2. **Every person listed on the poster participated in the unfunded AMSE
   effort and contributed to making the instrument, flight experiment,
   or original program happen** — as an unfunded, volunteer/student
   effort (see `docs/AMSE_PROGRAM_LOG.md`), authorship reflects
   participation in that effort as a whole, not a specific individually
   documented task, except where the poster text above specifically
   attributes a role.
3. **The September 2026 AI-assisted forensic reanalysis and re-reduction
   is later work that enables publication of this dataset; it does not
   displace or diminish the original team's contributions.** No person
   involved only in the 2026 re-reduction is implied to be an author by
   this document.
4. **Before submission, the team will confirm**: current affiliations
   and contact information for each listed author, final author order,
   each author's specific contribution role, and each author's approval
   of the manuscript and author list. This document is a preservation of
   the historical record, not a finalized submission author list.
